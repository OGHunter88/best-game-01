🔥 Emberfall Keep

The horde is coming down the ember road. The crystal must not fall.

Build towers beside the road, stop 12 waves of creatures, and keep the crystal alive. Spark is fast, Frost slows the pack, Cannon bursts them apart. Upgrade what works, sell what does not.

Build: pick a tower (keys 1–3), click a glowing cell beside the road. Click a tower to upgrade or sell it.

Waves: press Space or Start Wave when you are ready. Creatures that reach the crystal cost its health.

Win: survive all 12 waves, including the Ash Golem. Lose: the crystal hits 0❤.

Keys: 1/2/3 towers · Space start wave · P pause · F speed · M mute · R restart · Esc cancel.

Field notes

  • Frost first, then Cannon: slowed packs take the full blast.
  • Corners and long straights are worth the most — one tower covers more road.
  • Clearing a wave pays a bonus. Spend it before the next one.
  • Wave 12 brings the Ash Golem. Save something heavy for it.
